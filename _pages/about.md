Senior research engineer @ Cr3ate.AI
{:.role-tagline}

I like problems where geometry, optimization and learning meet. So far that has mostly meant 3D: structure from motion, neural rendering and Gaussian Splatting, from early research through to shipped product.

Lately I've been drawn to two directions: agents that reason over real-world scenes, and procedural generation of large, unbounded ones. Both come down to models that understand 3D worlds at scale.

Outside of work I help organize PSIML, a machine learning summer school, and study theoretical mathematics at the University of Belgrade.
