Work

  • 2021.11 - Present
    Senior Research Engineer
    Cr3ate.AI (formerly YDrive AI)
    • Structure from motion and 3D reconstruction
      • Designed and maintain the company's proprietary SfM pipeline. It scales from single objects and indoor spaces to multiple city blocks or 12 km of road, and reconstructs 60k images in 3D in under a day on a single consumer-level machine.
      • Extended it to a wide range of industrial and commercial cameras and 3D scanners, across driving, handheld, aerial, drone and mixed captures.
      • Scaled bundle adjustment with clustering algorithms and GPU-based solvers, which made SfM an order of magnitude faster.
      • Made the classical pipeline more robust with deep learning: learned mapping, camera calibration, gravity alignment and depth estimation.
    • Neural rendering and Gaussian Splatting
      • Built the company's real-time neural rendering system from scratch, with photorealistic renders of captured scenes in HD at 30 FPS. Shipped the first MVP to a customer.
      • Integrated it into Unreal Engine as a plugin with streaming to mobile devices, and delivered the first public demo of the technology on captures of Seattle.
      • Improved Gaussian Splatting rendering of views far from the captured ones, by distilling corrections from generative image and video models. Also researched densification strategies, both published and proprietary.
    • Scene understanding and spatial intelligence
      • Brought semantic understanding to reconstructed 3D scenes and shipped open-vocabulary segmentation as a product feature.
      • Working on R&D for the company's spatial intelligence product: AI agents that reason over captured 3D scenes. Prototyped and showcased it on a scan of a residential home under construction.
    • Mentorship and hiring
      • Designed the assignments and conducted the interviews for four intern hiring rounds.
      • Defined and supervised four research internships across neural rendering and Gaussian Splatting. One, a six-month project on generative 3D diffusion models, was run as a research collaboration with international 3D experts.
    • Customer and product work
      • Made the C++ pipeline releasable as a product, with Python bindings, Docker packaging and internal quality metrics.
      • Work directly with customers: support and feature planning for existing ones, tailored demos for prospective ones.
      • Co-led the company's exhibition at INTERGEO 2026, the leading geospatial conference: presented the product line to industry attendees and assessed market demand for 3D reconstruction and spatial intelligence.
